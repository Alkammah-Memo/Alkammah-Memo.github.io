Alkammah Memo – Privacy Policy

Alkammah Memo is a private executive notebook, task and calendar app used by its owner. This page explains how it handles Google account data.

What the app accesses

If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings, the app asks for permission to view and edit events on your Google Calendar and to see your email address. It uses this only to show your calendar events inside the app, to add, change or delete events when you do so in the app, and to show your tasks as calendar events.

How data is stored

Calendar events are kept in your own app workspace (on your device and in your own private cloud account). A Google "refresh token" that keeps the connection alive is stored in a private table that only the app's server function can read. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Your control

You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings → Google Calendar (this also revokes the token), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Alkammah Memo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
